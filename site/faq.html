FAQ

Quick answers about WxCareers.

Is there one site with meteorology jobs from every sector?

That's what WxCareers is for. Most boards cover one slice: USAJOBS is federal only, broadcast boards are TV only. WxCareers lists TV, government, private, aviation, military, and academic jobs together.

Is WxCareers legit?

Yes. It's free for job seekers and never asks you to pay. You apply on the employer's own site.

Is WxCareers part of Gray Media?

No. WxCareers isn't affiliated with any employer. The #wxcareers hashtag on LinkedIn is unrelated.

How is it different from the AMS Career Center?

AMS runs its own board. WxCareers pulls from many sources, including TV stations, NWS/USAJOBS, and private companies, so you can see more of the field in one search.

Where do I find entry-level jobs?

Filter by sector, or search for titles like "meteorologist intern" or "GS-5/7". Small-market TV and NWS openings are common first jobs.

How often is it updated?

Daily.
